Capture the web. Edit every detail. Free forever.

FrameShot is a free screen recorder and full page screenshot extension for Google Chrome. Record your screen with audio, your microphone and a webcam bubble, or grab an entire web page as one tall image in a single click. Then annotate it, blur private details, turn video clips into GIFs, and save everything as MP4, WebM, PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF.

There is no account to create, no watermark, no time limit and no paid upgrade. Everything runs locally in your browser, so your videos and images never leave your computer.

🚀 HOW TO USE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Take a full page screenshot in Chrome:
1. Pin FrameShot (puzzle-piece icon → pin) and open any page
2. Click the FrameShot icon and choose "Full page", or press Alt+Shift+F
3. Wait a moment while it scrolls; the result opens in the editor, ready to mark up, copy or download

Record your screen with sound:
1. Click the FrameShot icon and choose "Record screen"
2. Turn on tab or system sound, the microphone and the camera bubble if you want them
3. Press Start, pick a tab, window or screen in Chrome's dialog (keep "Also share tab audio" on to include a tab's sound), and stop when you're done; your MP4 is saved to your library

❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Is FrameShot really free?
Yes. Every feature is free, with no subscription, no trial and no watermark. Optional tips are welcome on the in-app Support page, but they unlock nothing.

Does it add a watermark to my videos or images?
Never.

Can it record computer or system audio?
It records a shared tab's sound on every system. When you share your entire screen, Chrome can include system sound on Windows and ChromeOS, and on macOS 14.2 or later with Chrome 141 or later; tick "Also share system audio" in Chrome's dialog. Chrome does not share sound from a single app window.

How long can a recording be?
There is no built-in time limit; it depends on your available disk space.

Where are my recordings and screenshots saved?
In FrameShot's private library on your computer, and in your Downloads folder whenever you download them.

Can I capture a page that needs a login?
Yes. FrameShot saves what your browser shows, including pages you are signed in to, without sending anything anywhere.

Does it work offline?
Yes. Capturing, recording, editing and exporting all work without an internet connection.

Good to know: Chrome does not let extensions work on its own settings pages (chrome://) or the Chrome Web Store, and it allows two snapshots per second, so very long pages take a few seconds. Blur can sometimes be reversed on short text, so use Redact for anything confidential.
